# Carrus

Latin for "cars." Carrus is a vehicle-rental locator platform that connects renters directly with rental partners based on location, budget, schedule, and vehicle fit — with transparent pricing and no hidden fees.

**Team:** Mateo Ortega, Sanjay Darshan Ramkumar, Ngoc Khanh Pham
**Course:** CMPE 131 — Software Engineering I, Fall 2026, SJSU

## Problem

People need temporary access to vehicles when they are traveling or when their personal transportation options are unavailable. Finding the perfect rental car that fits their location, budget, schedule, and style can often feel convoluted and complicated — comparison tools solve for price, but not for hidden fees, vehicle mismatch, or booking transparency.
